fried_egg

Description

fried_egg is a simple and delicious breakfast option. It can be used as a quick meal or served as a side dish. Depending on the cooking method, fried_egg can be crispy or soft and includes salt, pepper, and other seasonings. fried_egg can be eaten any time after especially after a meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

steps

  1. Heat the butter in a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Crack the eggs into a small bowl and whisk them together.
  3. Pour the eggs into the skillet.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Cook for 2-3 minutes, or until the edges are set.
